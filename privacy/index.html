Gulp — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

This policy covers Gulp, the game by CyberRoar (“we”, “us”) for Android, distributed on Google Play.

The short version

Gulp collects no personal data. It works completely offline: the game never connects to the internet and does not even ask Android for internet access. It has no ads, no accounts or sign-in, no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting SDKs and no in-app purchases. Everything the game saves stays on your device.

What Gulp stores on your device

  • Progress and settings. Your progress (Fish Bones, Hooks, The Great Net, unlocks, quests, Bestiary discoveries and run statistics) and your settings are saved as files in the game’s private storage on your phone. They are never sent anywhere. Gulp does not use Android’s cloud backup. When you move to a new phone, Android’s device-to-device transfer may copy the game’s files directly between your own devices; they never pass through us.
  • Optional playtest data — off by default. If you turn on Settings → Data & Privacy → Share anonymous play data, Gulp writes gameplay events to log files in its private storage, to help balance the game. Each entry holds the event name (for example “creature eaten” or “mutation chosen”), the seconds since the game started, a session number made at each launch from the date and time of that launch (UTC) and a random part, and gameplay ids and numbers (which creature, your size, which mutation, frame-time statistics); each file starts with the game version, the platform name and whether the build is a debug build. It contains no names, accounts, device identifiers, locale, location or free text. The game never sends these files anywhere. Turning the setting off stops new entries.
  • The Playtest report. Settings → Playtest report turns those files into balance numbers. If you tap Copy report, a short text report is placed on your clipboard. Besides the gameplay numbers and the days the logs cover, it lists performance facts about your device: the operating system and its version, the processor name and core count, the graphics chip and driver, the screen and window size, refresh rate and density, and the game and engine versions. Nothing leaves the game unless you paste that text into a message yourself; the app or service you paste it into then handles it under its own terms. Clear logs on the same screen deletes the playtest files.

Android keeps all of these files in the game’s private storage, which other apps cannot read.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep no game data: none of it ever reaches us. On your device the files stay until you delete them: Settings → Reset profile replaces your progress with a fresh profile (the old one stays behind as a single backup file until the game next saves), Playtest report → Clear logs erases the playtest files, and uninstalling Gulp (or clearing its storage in Android’s settings) deletes everything the game saved.

Permissions

Gulp asks Android for a single permission: vibrate, for optional haptic feedback (you can turn it off in Settings). It does not request internet access, location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files, or the advertising ID.

Third parties

Gulp includes no third-party services or SDKs that collect data: no advertising, analytics, attribution, social or crash-reporting libraries. It is built with the open-source Godot Engine, which does not collect data either.

Google Play handles downloading and updating the game and your Google account under Google’s Privacy Policy. If you have chosen to share usage and diagnostics data with Google, Google may show us crash reports and performance statistics about the game (Android vitals) without telling us who you are; this comes from Google, not from the game.

Testing programs and contacting us

If you join a test of Gulp on Google Play, we hold the email address you were invited with (testers are added to the test’s tester list in Play Console by email address) and receive any feedback you send through Google Play. If you email us, we receive your address and your message. We use these only to run the test and to reply to you, we never sell or share them, and we delete them when they are no longer needed for that purpose (on request too).

Children

Gulp does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes — for example if a future version adds an online feature — we will update this page and the date at the top before that version is released, and describe the change in the release notes.
